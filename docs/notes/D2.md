# D2: reward-regularised DMD2

`dmd2_reward` (`methods/dmd2_reward.py`) subclasses DMD2 and overrides only `generator_loss` and (advantage mode) `loss`. It does not touch `student_rollout` or the backward-simulation state.

- `mode: gradient`: `loss = loss_dmd2 - reward_weight * mean r(x0)`. The reward runs on the student's x0 in model space through `PixelCodec.decode_tensor` (same clamp and shift as `decode`) and the reward's `torch_call`. Rewards with a torch path set `differentiable = True` (`channel_dominance`, `brightness`, `temporal_smoothness`, and `composite` when all terms are).
- `mode: advantage`: `group_size` student rollouts share a prompt; the reward is evaluated on decoded numpy samples; the DMD2 per-sample gradient is scaled by `g * softmax(reward_weight * A)` within the group, with A the detached group-normalised advantage. Mean weight is 1. Not run in the experiment below.
- `reward_weight = 0` reproduces DMD2 exactly (tested bit-for-bit for both modes).

## Experiment (CPU, 300 student steps, batch 32, 4-step student, scored at NFE 4, n=256)

Teacher `runs/image_flow_grpo/sft/final`, reward `channel_dominance` channel 0, gradient mode. Weight 0 is plain DMD2 through the same code path.

| weight | reward | precision | recall | fd | class_acc |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| teacher, 32 steps | 0.030 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0 | 0.930 |
| teacher, 4 steps | 0.042 | 0.949 | 0.875 | 0.231 | 0.883 |
| 0 | 0.036 | 0.934 | 0.914 | 0.309 | 0.984 |
| 1 | 0.092 | 0.883 | 0.715 | 0.634 | 0.707 |
| 4 | 0.968 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 35.98 | 0.234 |

Reward rises with the weight and precision/recall fall. Weight 1 gives 2.5x the reward for about 0.2 recall. Weight 4 is reward hacking: diversity 0.013, collapsed to a saturated image. A useful weight lies between 0 and 1. Single seed, short run, three points.
