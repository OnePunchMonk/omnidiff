# D2: reward-regularised DMD2

`dmd2_reward` (`methods/dmd2_reward.py`) subclasses DMD2 and overrides `setup`, `loss` (student role) and `generator_loss`. It does not touch `student_rollout` or the stage selection. The reward applies to the student's x0 prediction at whichever stage was drawn (including `last_stage_prob`).

- `mode: gradient`: `loss = loss_dmd2 - reward_weight * mean r(x0)`. The reward runs on the student's x0 in model space through `PixelCodec.decode_tensor` (same clamp and shift as `decode`, tested) and the reward's `torch_call`. Rewards with a torch path set `differentiable = True` (`channel_dominance`, `brightness`, `temporal_smoothness`, and `composite` when all terms are). The numpy path is unchanged.
- `mode: advantage`: `group_size` student rollouts share a prompt; the reward is evaluated on decoded numpy samples; the DMD2 per-sample gradient is scaled by `g * softmax(reward_weight * A)` within the group, with A the detached group-normalised advantage. Mean weight is 1. Not run in the experiment below.
- The reward is built with the trainer's context and receives the sampled prompts.
- At `reward_weight = 0` the generator loss and gradient equal DMD2's for a given x0, and `loss("student")` equals DMD2's (same prompts, same rng use; no grouping). Both are tested.
- The reward and DMD2 gradients are not on a common scale. The metric `reward_grad_ratio` logs `weight * mean|dr/dx0|` over the DMD2 gradient's mean magnitude on x0.

## Experiment

CPU, gradient mode, `channel_dominance` channel 0, teacher `runs/image_flow_grpo/sft/final`, config `configs/distill/image_flow_dmd2_reward.yaml` (with `last_stage_prob: 0.5`) with the override `distill.steps=300` (config default is 600; `eval_every=1000` to skip mid-run eval). Batch 32, 4-step student, scored at NFE 4, n=256, seed 0. One seed. Weight 0 is plain DMD2 through the same code path. Raw output: `docs/notes/D2_sweep/score.json`, `score.md`, `train_w*.log`.

| weight | reward | precision | recall | fd | diversity | class_acc | reward_grad_ratio (end of training) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| teacher, 32 steps | 0.030 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0 | 0.164 | 0.930 | |
| teacher, 4 steps | 0.042 | 0.949 | 0.875 | 0.231 | 0.151 | 0.883 | |
| 0 | 0.028 | 0.953 | 0.934 | 0.147 | 0.168 | 0.977 | 0 |
| 0.1 | 0.031 | 0.938 | 0.934 | 0.148 | 0.168 | 0.973 | 0.15 |
| 0.3 | 0.042 | 0.930 | 0.953 | 0.138 | 0.165 | 0.965 | 0.43 |
| 1 | 0.143 | 0.863 | 0.641 | 1.025 | 0.160 | 0.570 | 0.85 |

Reward is on a [-1, 1] scale. Relative to weight 0: weight 0.3 gives reward +0.014 with no loss in recall or fd; weight 1 gives +0.115 but recall falls by 0.29, fd rises about 7x (0.147 to 1.025) and class_acc falls from 0.977 to 0.570. Between 0.3 and 1 the student leaves the data distribution. (An earlier run without `last_stage_prob`, 300 steps, weights 0/1/4, gave the same pattern, with weight 4 collapsing to one saturated image: reward 0.97, precision and recall 0.)

One seed, 300 steps, four weights; differences among weights 0 to 0.3 are within what I would expect from seed noise at n=256.
